## 2. 🧠 Why Other Agencies Don’t Build This (The 3 Moats)

1. **Severe Skillset Mismatch:**  
   95% of digital and social media marketing agencies in Dubai are staffed by creatives, videographers, media buyers, and account managers. They do not employ systems or data engineers capable of building low-level SIMD byte scanners or async proxy rotators.
2. **Anti-Bot & Maintenance Overhead:**  
   Scraping directories and live websites requires bypassing Web Application Firewalls (Cloudflare, Akamai) and adapting to frequent DOM changes. Agencies lack the engineering resources to maintain scrapers.
3. **Core Business Focus:**  
   Agencies make money delivering client work ($5k–$20k/mo). They treat internal scraping as an annoying technical chore and will gladly pay a monthly subscription for clean, pre-verified data.

## 6. 🏗️ The #1 Pure Scraper Goldmine: Commercial Office Fit-Out

* **The Mandatory Need:** When a company registers a trade license with a physical address in a commercial tower (*Business Bay, DIFC, JLT, d3*), the office is handed over as an **empty concrete shell (Shell & Core)**.
* **The Legal Requirement:** The business **must hire a fit-out contractor** within 30–60 days to install flooring, glass partitions, HVAC, ceilings, and MEP cabling before they can operate.
* **The Revenue:**
  * Average fit-out project: **AED 250,000 – AED 1,000,000+**.
  * Contractors make **25% – 35% gross margin**.
  * **Contractors pay:** **AED 1,000 – AED 2,000 per verified commercial tenant lead**, or a **3%–5% referral commission (AED 15,000 – AED 40,000 per closed deal)**.
